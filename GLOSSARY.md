# creative-skills

Craft knowledge from image, video, 3D and music production, written as skills an agent can apply, learned and recorded one craft at a time.

## Knowledge

**Craft**:
What makes creative work good regardless of the tool that made it, such as why a low angle makes a subject feel powerful.
_Avoid_: technique, best practice

**Process**:
The sequence that moves a piece from idea to finished asset: brief, reference, draft, critique, revise, deliver.
_Avoid_: workflow, pipeline

**Tooling**:
How a specific model, app or API wants to be asked for something. It changes far faster than craft.
_Avoid_: prompt engineering

**Critique**:
Judging a piece of work against its brief with a precise vocabulary, then naming what to change.
_Avoid_: review, feedback, evaluation

**Brief**:
The statement of what a piece must achieve and for whom; the standard that critique measures against.
_Avoid_: spec, prompt, requirements

## Organization

**Skill**:
One craft or process, written so an agent can apply it.

**Category**:
A group of skills that are installed together.
_Avoid_: pack, collection

**Foundations**:
The category for craft that holds the same across two or more media, plus critique.
_Avoid_: fundamentals, basics, direction

**Medium**:
One kind of output: image, video, 3D or music. Craft tied to a single medium belongs to that medium's category.
_Avoid_: modality, format

**Study notes**:
The raw material written while learning a craft, kept apart from skills and never given to an agent.

## Roles

**Director**:
The role that owns what the work is: its vision, look, sound and feel.

**Producer**:
The role that owns whether the work gets made: budget, schedule, people, rights and delivery.

## Production phases

**Development**:
Finding the idea worth making and deciding to make it.

**Pre-production**:
Locking every decision possible before making anything: script, storyboard, style, plan.

**Production**:
Making the raw material by shooting, recording, modeling or generating.

**Post-production**:
Turning raw material into the finished piece: edit, grade, sound, mix.

**Delivery**:
Packaging the finished piece to every required spec and clearing it to ship.

**Publication**:
Putting the piece in front of an audience and learning from the response.

**Take**:
One attempt at a shot, recording or generation. Generations are takes: select among them against the brief.
_Avoid_: variation, sample, output
