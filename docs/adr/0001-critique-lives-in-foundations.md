# Critique lives in foundations, not a separate category

Critique skills (judging work against a brief, art direction, series consistency) sit in `foundations` alongside craft skills like composition and lighting, rather than in a separate `direction` category. Art education teaches it this way: foundation courses include critique as part of learning the craft, and no standard text treats "direction" as a category of theory. The cost is that critique can't be installed without the craft skills, which is acceptable because critique uses their vocabulary anyway.

## Considered options

- **Separate `direction` category:** made critique more visible as the area no vendor skill repo covers, and installable alone. Rejected because the split was our invention, not established terminology.
- **Naming the category `fundamentals`:** the word working artists use, but it suggests beginner material, which fits critique poorly.
